About Us

We are fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students from Centro Escolar University – Malolos, dedicated to exploring the potential of technology and interactive digital experiences. As part of our academic capstone, we developed CodeBound, a web-based educational video game designed to enhance Senior High School students’ understanding of fundamental programming concepts through a game-based learning experience.

Our project applies game-based learning to provide an interactive and engaging approach towards programming fundamentals. We integrated educational content with gameplay so that it aims to help students develop a foundational understanding of programming while strengthening their logical reasoning and problem-solving skills. Beyond fulfilling our academic requirements, we aim to create a project that offers meaningful value to students and educators. CodeBound is intended to serve as a supplementary educational resource that provides an alternative approach to learning the basics of programming and demonstrates the potential of educational materials being gamified.

We are the team behind IP Games, a student-led development group whose name reflects our beginnings as a circle of friends, all with a shared vision of success. Through CodeBound, we will apply our knowledge and technical skills to develop meaningful digital solutions that extend beyond academic research and contribute to the learning experiences of others.
